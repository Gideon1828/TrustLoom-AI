SECTION: TOOLKIT FEATURES
TOPIC: PDF Download, Candidate Comparison, Interview Questions, New Evaluation

--- TOOLKIT OVERVIEW ---
The Toolkit section on the Results page provides 5 action cards:
1. Download Report (PDF)
2. Score Explanations (XAI)
3. Compare Candidates
4. Interview Questions
5. New Evaluation

Each card has a title, description, and a category badge (Export, AI, or Compare).

--- 1. DOWNLOAD REPORT (PDF) ---
PURPOSE: Generate a downloadable PDF report of the evaluation.

HOW TO USE:
1. Click the "Download Report" card in the Toolkit section.
2. A modal dialog opens where you can enter the Candidate Name (default is "Candidate").
3. Click "Download PDF" to generate and download the report.
4. Click "Cancel" to close without downloading.

WHAT THE PDF INCLUDES:
• Trust score and risk assessment
• Detailed score breakdown (all components)
• Observations and flags
• Metadata and timestamp
• The PDF is generated locally in your browser — no data is sent to a server.

--- 2. SCORE EXPLANATIONS (XAI) ---
PURPOSE: Toggle the detailed AI explanations section.

HOW TO USE:
1. Click the "Score Explanations" card.
2. The XAI explanations section will appear below the charts.
3. Click again to hide the section.
4. The card shows "Currently Showing" when explanations are visible.
(See the XAI Explanations knowledge base section for more details.)

--- 3. COMPARE CANDIDATES ---
PURPOSE: Compare the current candidate with 1 or 2 additional resumes side-by-side.

HOW TO USE:
1. Click the "Compare Candidates" card.
2. A modal wizard opens with steps:
   Step 1 (SELECT): Choose whether to compare with 1 additional resume (2-way) or 2 additional resumes (3-way).
   Step 2 (UPLOAD): Upload the additional resume file(s) (PDF or DOCX, max 10 MB each).
   Step 3 (PROCESSING): Wait while the system evaluates the additional resumes.
   Step 4 (RESULTS): View the comparison table.
3. If there is an error, you will see an error screen with a "Retry" button.

WHAT THE COMPARISON SHOWS:
• Side-by-side candidate scores
• A winner determination (which candidate scored highest)
• Score differences between candidates
• Summary text comparing strengths

--- 4. INTERVIEW QUESTIONS ---
PURPOSE: Generate AI-tailored interview questions based on the candidate's profile.

HOW TO USE:
1. Click the "Interview Questions" card.
2. Wait while questions are generated (shows a spinner with "Generating...").
3. The interview questions panel appears.

WHAT YOU GET:
• 10 questions total in 3 categories:
  - 4 Technical Questions — with expected answers and skill tags
  - 3 General Questions — with expected answers (behavioral/situational)
  - 3 Project Questions — without answers (specific to the candidate's projects)
• Each question shows a difficulty badge: Junior, Mid, or Senior (color-coded).
• Click a question to expand and see the expected answer (Technical and General only).
• Copy button — copies a single question and answer to clipboard.
• "Copy All" button — copies all questions.
• "Regenerate" button — generates a fresh set of questions.
• "Close" button — hides the panel.

--- 5. NEW EVALUATION ---
PURPOSE: Start a fresh evaluation from scratch.

HOW TO USE:
1. Click the "New Evaluation" card.
2. You are taken back to the input form with all fields cleared.
3. The previous results are no longer displayed.
