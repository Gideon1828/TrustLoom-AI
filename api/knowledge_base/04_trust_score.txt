SECTION: TRUST SCORE AND RESULTS
TOPIC: Understanding the Trust Score, Score Breakdown, Risk Levels

--- TRUST SCORE ---
• The Trust Score is a number from 0 to 100 that represents the overall trustworthiness of a freelancer's profile.
• It is displayed as a large circular gauge on the Results page with a color-coded ring:
  - Green (80–100): Excellent trustworthiness
  - Yellow (55–79): Good or Average trustworthiness
  - Red (0–54): Needs Attention — significant concerns found

--- RISK LEVELS ---
Three risk levels are determined by the trust score:
• LOW RISK (score 80–100) → Recommendation: TRUSTWORTHY
  "Shows strong indicators of trustworthiness. High credibility and low risk factors."
• MEDIUM RISK (score 55–79) → Recommendation: MODERATE
  "Shows moderate trustworthiness. Consider reviewing specific flags."
• HIGH RISK (score 0–54) → Recommendation: RISKY
  "Shows significant risk factors. Careful consideration and thorough verification recommended."

--- SCORE COMPONENTS ---
The trust score is made up of three main components:

1. LANGUAGE QUALITY (0–25 points)
   • Analyzes the resume text for professional language, action verbs, grammar, and writing quality.
   • Higher scores mean the resume is well-written and uses professional terminology.
   • Lower scores may indicate poor writing, generic text, or AI-generated filler.

2. PROJECT REALISM (0–45 points)
   • Analyzes project descriptions in the resume for realistic patterns.
   • Checks project timelines, overlaps, duration consistency, and technology usage.
   • Higher scores mean the projects appear genuine and consistent.
   • Lower scores may indicate fabricated projects, impossible timelines, or suspicious patterns.

3. PROFILE VALIDATION (0–30 points)
   This is further broken down:
   • GitHub Validation (0–10 points): Checks GitHub profile existence, activity, repositories, contributions.
   • LinkedIn Validation (0–10 points): Checks LinkedIn profile existence and completeness.
   • Portfolio (0–5 points): If provided, checks if the portfolio website is accessible and relevant.
   • Experience Match (0–5 points): Compares the claimed experience level against what is found in the resume and profiles. Full match = 5, partial match = 3, mismatch = 0.

--- SCORE CALCULATION SUMMARY ---
• Resume Quality (Language + Projects) = 0 to 70 points
• Profile Validation (GitHub + LinkedIn + Portfolio + Experience) = 0 to 30 points
• TOTAL TRUST SCORE = 0 to 100 points

--- WHAT IS DISPLAYED ON THE RESULTS PAGE ---
1. Hero Section: Large trust score circle, risk level badge, recommendation card.
2. Quick Stats Strip: Three quick numbers — Language Quality (/25), Project Realism (/45), Profile Validation (/30).
3. Score Breakdown: Detailed progress bars for each component, showing score, maximum, and percentage.
4. Summary Table: Tabular view with Resume Quality (/70), Profile Validation (/30), and Final Trust Score (/100).
5. Visual Analytics: Three charts (doughnut chart, bar chart, component comparison chart).

--- HOW TO IMPROVE THE SCORE ---
• Check the "Improvement Suggestions" section for specific, actionable tips.
• Each suggestion shows how many points you could gain.
• Common improvements:
  - Improve resume writing quality (more action verbs, quantified achievements)
  - Add more detailed project descriptions with realistic timelines
  - Make your GitHub profile active (more repositories, recent commits)
  - Complete your LinkedIn profile
  - Add a portfolio website
  - Ensure experience level matches resume content
