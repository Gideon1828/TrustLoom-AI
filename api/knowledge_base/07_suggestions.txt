SECTION: IMPROVEMENT SUGGESTIONS
TOPIC: How to Improve Trust Score, Actionable Suggestions

--- WHAT ARE IMPROVEMENT SUGGESTIONS? ---
• After an evaluation, the system generates specific, actionable tips to improve the trust score.
• Each suggestion tells you exactly what to do and how many points you could gain.
• Some suggestions are enhanced by AI for more detailed advice.

--- WHERE TO FIND SUGGESTIONS ---
• On the Results page, look for the "Improvement Suggestions" section.
• It appears when there are suggestions to show.
• A trigger banner says: "We found X actionable suggestions that could improve the trust score by up to +Y points."
• Click the banner or scroll down to see all suggestions.

--- SUGGESTION SUMMARY ---
At the top of the suggestions section, you see 3 stats:
• Total number of suggestions
• Total potential points gain (how many points the score could improve)
• Possible new score (current score + potential gain, capped at 100)

--- SUGGESTION CATEGORIES ---
Suggestions are organized into 4 categories:

1. LANGUAGE QUALITY
   • Tips to improve resume writing, add action verbs, quantify achievements.
   • Example: "Use more specific action verbs like 'developed', 'implemented', 'designed' instead of passive language."

2. PROJECT PATTERNS
   • Tips to improve project descriptions, fix timeline issues.
   • Example: "Add specific project durations and avoid overlapping timelines."

3. PROFILE LINKS
   • Tips to improve GitHub, LinkedIn, and portfolio profiles.
   • Example: "Increase GitHub activity by pushing commits regularly. Add more detailed repository descriptions."

4. EXPERIENCE MATCH
   • Tips to align claimed experience with resume content.
   • Example: "Your resume mentions entry-level tasks but you selected Senior Level. Ensure consistency."

--- EACH SUGGESTION CARD SHOWS ---
• Category icon and title
• "AI Enhanced" badge — if the suggestion was enhanced by AI for more detail
• The original flag that triggered the suggestion
• Impact badge — shows potential point gain (e.g., "+3 points")
• Priority level — High, Medium, or Low (with color-coded icons)
• Suggestion text — the main recommendation
• Action steps — a checklist of specific steps to take (you can check them off as you complete them)
• Examples — before/after comparisons showing what good vs. bad looks like

--- CONTROLS ---
• "Expand All" — opens all suggestion cards at once
• "Collapse All" — closes all suggestion cards
• Click any suggestion card to expand/collapse it individually

--- TIPS FOR IMPROVING SCORES ---
1. Language Quality: Use professional language, include specific metrics ("increased sales by 40%"), avoid generic phrases.
2. Projects: Include realistic project descriptions with clear timelines, technologies used, and outcomes.
3. GitHub: Keep your GitHub active — regular commits, well-documented repositories, meaningful README files.
4. LinkedIn: Complete your LinkedIn profile — professional photo, detailed experience, skills, recommendations.
5. Portfolio: Add a portfolio website showcasing your best work with descriptions and links.
6. Experience: Make sure the experience level you select matches what your resume actually demonstrates.
