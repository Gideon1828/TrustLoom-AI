SECTION: AUTHENTICATION AND ACCOUNT
TOPIC: Login, Registration, Password, OAuth, Account Management

--- LOGIN ---
• Go to the login page at /login.
• Enter your email and password, then click "Sign In."
• You can also sign in with Google or GitHub using the social login buttons.
• "Remember me" keeps your session active longer.
• If you enter wrong credentials, you will see "Invalid login credentials."
• After too many failed attempts, you will see "Too many login attempts. Please try again later."
• If your session expires, you will be redirected to login with a message "Your session has expired. Please sign in again."

--- REGISTRATION ---
• Go to /register or click "Create an account" from the login page.
• Required fields:
  - Full Name (minimum 2 characters)
  - Email (valid email address)
  - Password (minimum 8 characters, must contain uppercase letter, lowercase letter, and a number)
  - Confirm Password (must match)
  - Accept Terms & Conditions (checkbox)
• Optional field: Organization.
• A password strength meter shows Weak / Fair / Good / Strong as you type.
• You can also register with Google or GitHub.
• After registering, you may need to check your email to confirm your account before logging in.

--- FORGOT PASSWORD ---
• Click "Forgot password?" on the login page.
• Enter your email address and click "Send Reset Link."
• Check your email for the reset instructions.
• If no account exists with that email, you will see "No account found with this email address."

--- GOOGLE / GITHUB LOGIN ---
• Click the Google or GitHub button on the login or register page.
• You will be redirected to the provider's login page.
• After granting permission, you are redirected back to TrustLoom and signed in automatically.
• OAuth users can later add a password from Profile Settings → Password tab.

--- DELETE ACCOUNT ---
• Open the user menu (click your avatar in the top-right corner).
• Click "Delete Account" (red icon at the bottom of the menu).
• A confirmation dialog will appear. This action is permanent and cannot be undone.
• Deleting your account removes your profile, evaluation history, and profile picture.

--- SIGN OUT ---
• Open the user menu and click "Sign Out" at the bottom.
• You will be redirected to the login page.
