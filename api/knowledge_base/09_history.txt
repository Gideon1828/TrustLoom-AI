SECTION: EVALUATION HISTORY
TOPIC: Saved Evaluations, History Sidebar, Loading Past Results

--- WHAT IS EVALUATION HISTORY? ---
• Every evaluation you complete is automatically saved.
• You can access past evaluations from the History sidebar on the left side of the dashboard.
• History is only available when you are signed in.

--- HOW TO OPEN THE HISTORY SIDEBAR ---
1. On the Dashboard, look for the hamburger menu icon (☰) in the top-left corner.
2. Click it to open the sidebar.
3. Click the X button or click outside the sidebar to close it.

--- HOW EVALUATIONS ARE ORGANIZED ---
Evaluations are grouped by time:
• Today
• Yesterday
• Previous 7 Days
• Previous 30 Days
• Older

Each group can be collapsed or expanded by clicking the arrow (▼).

--- WHAT EACH EVALUATION ITEM SHOWS ---
• A type icon:
  - 📄 — Standard evaluation
  - ⚖️ — Comparison evaluation
  - ❓ — Interview questions session
• Title text (usually the resume file name or a custom name)
• Score badge showing the percentage

--- ACTIONS ON EACH EVALUATION ---
Click the three-dot menu (⋯) on any evaluation to see:
• ✏️ Rename — Change the title of the evaluation. An inline text input appears; press Enter to save or Escape to cancel.
• 🗑️ Delete — Remove the evaluation. A confirmation dialog appears asking "Are you sure?" before deletion.

--- LOADING A PAST EVALUATION ---
• Click on any evaluation in the sidebar to load its results.
• The Results page will display the saved data.

--- PAGINATION ---
• The sidebar loads 20 evaluations at a time.
• If you have more, a "Load More" button appears at the bottom.

--- STARTING A NEW EVALUATION ---
• Click the "+ New Evaluation" button at the top of the sidebar.
• This takes you back to the input form.

--- SIDEBAR FOOTER ---
• Shows your avatar (initials), name, and email.
• Sign-out button (🚪) at the bottom.

--- NOT SIGNED IN ---
• If you're not signed in, the sidebar shows: "Sign in to save and view your evaluation history" with a "Sign In" link.

--- EMPTY STATE ---
• If you have no saved evaluations: "No evaluations yet. Upload a resume to get started!"
